Goals:
Primary: find and keep track of news items related to developments that are significant to defined interests.

Functional:
	daily refresh (2-3 am ET)
	evolve the topics over time
	
	One potentially interesting direction: 
		I imagine having a private version based on my own personal interests
		And building a public versions, e.g. that
			aggregate global, national and local news 
			industry-specific news
		These could then be "stacked" together into a single personalized view, ranking them based on my own personal weightings
		
Agent role: everything -- scraper, aggregator, editor, operator and interface

Input: 
	rss feeds may or may not be useful
	news items generally available to the agent (this is where I think we may be able to dramatically improve the structure; given the news access that foundational models seem to have now, I don't know if the feeds are necessary; or perhaps they're good as pointers, but shouldn't be used exclusively)
	Sources specific to my interests
	Sources behind paywalls, especially:
		NYT (where I have a subscription)
		Economist (where I have a subscription)
		My LinkedIn feed (I'm particularly interested in this because this is where I find the most pertinent developments to my industry, and prescient anlysis of trends)
	Email newsletters
	last time, I tried to boil the ocean from the start, incorporating a broad range of feeds from the start. 
	I'd like to take a more incremental approach here and prove the utility of the concept & format and incrementally add more sources.

Output

	Summaries, linked to full details
	This could be anything: a simple webpage, an app, markdown files, something I'm not imagining -- whatever's most well suited to the task.
