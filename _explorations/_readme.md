# Explorations

Open thinking. Transcripts, brainstorms, riffs, and half-formed threads that are not yet work.

This is unscoped activity. The underscore marks it as not canon, the same as `_active-projects/` and `_archive/`; what separates it from `_active-projects/` is that an exploration has no scope, no plan, and no definition of done. It carries no tracker by design. See the two classes in the drive map.

Nothing here is canonical, and nothing here should be cited as though it were. An exploration is thinking in progress, including the parts that turn out to be wrong.

## How it works

One directory per exploration, named `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug`. The date is the day the thinking started, and it does not change as the directory accumulates.

Inside, whatever the thinking needs. Source material first (a transcript, a paper, a set of notes), then the riff. No required file structure. Source material keeps the corpus naming convention where it has an author: `topic — author — YYYY-MM`.

An exploration can sit unfinished forever. That is a normal state here, not a loose end. One that goes dormant with a known restart condition gets an orientation note, `_state-of-the-exploration — YYYY-MM-DD.md`, and a Someday Maybe bullet in the root tracker naming the promotion gate.

## What leaves, and how

Three exits, and most explorations take the third.

- **It becomes work.** The exploration produced intent. Open a project in `_active-projects/` with a plan and a tracker, and point the project plan back at the exploration directory as its origin. The exploration stays where it is; it is the record of how the project got its shape.
- **It produced knowledge.** A durable document graduates to its topical home under the usual test: would a future session look for this without knowing the exploration existed? Then it moves.
- **It stays.** Most thinking is not finished thinking. Nothing needs to happen.

An exploration that closes (it became a project, or a decision ended it) gets a closing record, `_closing-record — YYYY-MM-DD.md`, saying where everything went, and moves to `_archive/` keeping its dated slug. Explorations are not archived on a schedule. If one is genuinely dead, delete it.

## The one rule that reaches outside

Anything surfaced here that reads as intent goes to the root tracker, SFL if it is intended and out of scope, Someday Maybe if it is merely a possibility. Without that, the trackers stop being the full picture of what is in flight, and an idea that mattered gets buried in a directory nobody reopens. Promotion to MTG stays Patrick's.
